# Scheduler Version 2

## Goal

Generate a conflict-free university timetable.

## Hard Constraints

- Teacher cannot teach two classes simultaneously.
- Room cannot be double-booked.
- Section cannot attend two classes simultaneously.
- Lab activities require exactly two consecutive periods.
- Labs must be scheduled only in laboratory rooms.
- Room capacity must satisfy section strength.
- Weekly hours must be fully allocated.
- Teacher unavailable slots must be respected.

## Soft Constraints

- Balanced faculty workload.
- Minimize idle periods.
- Spread lectures across the week.
- Reduce room movement.
- Better room utilization.

## Scheduling Order

1. Fixed Events
2. Labs
3. Shared Faculty
4. Large Sections
5. Lectures
6. Tutorials

Recursive Backtracking
